Framing
The Last Piece of the Foundation

Before every calculation — a look inward, and a look at the bigger picture.

Every financial decision is shaped as much by habits and emotions as by technical knowledge. And every individual decision happens inside a national context — one where access to finance has changed dramatically in the last two decades.

💡 Why This Comes Last in Module 0Money scripts — unconscious beliefs about money formed early in life — quietly influence spending and saving habits for years. Naming your own patterns now, before the rest of this curriculum builds on them, makes every later module land differently.

Key facts to emphasise:
• All figures verified against the Bank of Zambia Governor's own 2025 FinScope Survey launch remarks
• The youth gap (4.9pp) is now larger than the gender gap (2.2pp) — a genuinely useful, non-obvious fact to highlight
